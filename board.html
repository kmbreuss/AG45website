The AG45 Board

The right people at the table.

Every AG45 engagement is shaped by the room around it. The Board is who AG45 is — operators, strategists, and specialists who bring board-level discipline to owner-led businesses.

Founder & CEO

Katherine Breuss

AG45 — Mequon, Wisconsin

Katherine has spent more than 25 years as an operator, advisor, and strategist across the U.S., the U.K., Asia Pacific, and Australia. Her strength is discernment through chaos and change — knowing what's actually happening underneath the noise, and knowing who needs to be at the table to do something about it. She founded AG45 in 2023 to build the firm she wished she'd had access to twenty-five years ago.

The Advisors

A Board built around the work.

Each advisor brings a specific function — and the discipline to make it count when an owner needs it.

Leighann Lovely

Sales Advisor

Leighann is the Founder of Love Your Sales, a fractional sales practice for small and mid-sized businesses. With twenty years across human resources, staffing, and business development, she brings the human side of sales — guiding clients through the decision-making process that turns conversations into committed work. She is the host of the Love Your Sales Podcast and was named to Marquis Who's Who in America in 2023.

Krystle Rogers

Lead Strategist

Krystle is a strategist, certified coach (ACC, M.Ed.), and entrepreneur who supports owners and organizations in building capacity, making aligned decisions, and driving sustainable growth. She is co-owner of Best Ed Business, an enterprise specializing in strategic sourcing and supply for community-focused organizations. Her practice combines learning architecture with leadership development — translating insight into action while keeping the long view.

Ari Milner

Technology Advisor

Ari is the Owner of PlanIT, where he has spent the past seven years guiding businesses through technology decisions and digital infrastructure. With eighteen years across IT, websites, and online marketing — and three startups behind him — Ari serves as a Virtual Chief Information Officer (vCIO) for clients who need technology strategy without a full-time CTO. He brings the eye that sees what the system actually needs to do — and the hands to build it.
