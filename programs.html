Program 01

The Ownership
Blueprint™

Solo · Digital · 45 days

Yourself first. Second your business. Because it starts with you.

Most owners arrive having done some version of personal work — a coach, a book, a retreat. The work touches the same topics: direction, identity, values. But it lands somewhere different. AG45's owner spine isn't the destination. It's the foundation.

The Ownership Blueprint™ is a self-directed digital application of the True Ownership™ methodology — the same five disciplines AG45 applies in its flagship engagements, structured into a 45-day digital experience for solopreneurs and owners ready to do the foundational work themselves.

Phase 01 · Own It
The owner spine.
Forty-five days at the questions most owners have circled but few have landed: what you're actually building, who you are when the work gets hard, what you'll say no to. The output isn't insight — it's an operating system, documented and referable.
Phase 02 · Build It
The business spine.
Once Phase 1 is documented, Phase 2 builds the business architecture that fits what you're actually building. Strategy, structure, and operating systems — connected to the owner spine, so the business inherits your clarity, not your defaults.
Program 01 · Inside the Blueprint

Two phases. One Blueprint.

Phase 1 builds the owner spine. Phase 2 builds the business spine on top of it. Each phase stands on its own; together they produce the full Blueprint.

Program 02

The Owner's
Circle™

Group of 4 · Strategist-led · 12-month commitment

Four non-competing owners. One AG45 strategist. The room becomes the Board.

You're the leader and the operator. The room changes that. Most peer groups are masterminds — neutral facilitators keeping the conversation going. The Circle is different. An AG45 strategist leads the room, applying the True Ownership™ methodology to each member's work.

A small group is the point. Four members, not eight. Non-competing businesses, not adjacent. Strategist-led, not facilitated. The composition is what makes the Circle work — and what makes AG45 selective about who gets in. The Circle is a 12-month commitment — because real progress compounds, and the work isn't a one-and-done event. Application and fit call required.

Session 01 · Forward
Priorities and pattern recognition.
Each owner brings the priorities they're working through. The room responds — questions, perspectives, pattern recognition from across industries. The strategist holds the methodology.
Session 02 · Back
Accountability and follow-through.
Each owner brings what they did since last session. The room holds them accountable — for commitments made, work attempted, results produced. Honest follow-through, in front of peers.

The structure that turns a peer group into a Board — and a Board into accountable strategic discipline.

01
The True Owner's Audit™ as the entry.
Free, light, self-directed. Frames where you are across the 5A Framework before the engagement begins.

An AG45 strategist leading the room.
Not a neutral facilitator. The strategist applies the True Ownership™ methodology to each member's work, surfaces patterns, holds the discipline.
03
Three peer owners. Non-competing businesses.
Four total members in the Circle. Curated by AG45 for strategic seriousness and willingness to act.

Quarterly State-of-the-Business presentations.

06
AG45 app integration.
Commitments tracked across the cohort. Each member's progress visible to the room. Accountability becomes structural.
Program 03

Strategic
Cadence™

1:1 · Monthly · 12-month minimum

One strategist who knows your business deeply. Board-level advisors behind the scene.

The bridge between peer challenge and full Strategic Council work. For owners who want a senior strategist who knows the business deeply — and the structural support of AG45's broader bench, without managing it as separate engagements. The complexity stays behind the scene.

A single relationship at the center: you and your Lead Strategist, working monthly on strategic priorities, leadership decisions, and structural choices. When the work calls for specialist depth, your strategist pulls in advisors from AG45's broader bench. You don't manage them. Your strategist does.

Monthly
Working sessions.
Two-hour 1:1s with your Lead Strategist. Pre-work expected; documentation produced. The discipline of monthly strategic conversation that no business naturally creates on its own.
Quarterly · Annually
Strategic reviews.
Deeper sessions where strategic patterns become visible and decisions get made. Annual full refresh — direction recommitted to or reset, next twelve months planned with clear priorities.

Program 04 · Flagship

Leader Run.
Owner Free.

Multi-year · Board of Advisors · $10M+ businesses

A multi-year engagement — typically 3 to 5 years — that prepares the owner to break free of the business. Succession, sale, or going passive while retaining ownership.

Most advisory engagements give you one strategist. Leader Run™ gives you a Board. A Lead Strategist anchors the relationship; a team of advisors is assembled around them — drawn from across functional, financial, transactional, and external-relationship specialties. The composition depends on the business.

The work doesn't optimize for one outcome. It prepares the business — and the owner — for whichever path fits their life, their family, and their economics. The choice belongs to the owner, not the firm. Three legitimate paths: pass it on, sell it, or stay involved without operating.

Stage 01
Foundation.
Strategic direction set. Operating rhythm installed. The Board of Advisors assembles and embeds with the leadership team. The audit informs which specialists get drawn in.
Stage 02 · 03
Transition. Then options.
The owner's work shifts. Systems get automated; decisions get delegated; the team takes on owner-mindset accountability. Stage 3 integrates strategy and operations fully — and the path forward gets chosen.
Program 04 · Inside Leader Run

Six elements. Three stages. One arc.

A multi-year engagement built around the owner — with a Board of Advisors and the firm's full methodology applied at the highest altitude.

01
1:1 with the Lead Strategist.
The owner's direct strategic counterpart for the duration of the engagement. Regular working sessions, asynchronous access, documented decisions. The relationship that anchors everything else.
02
Board of Advisors assembled around the owner.
Lead Strategist plus advisors drawn from functional, financial, transactional, and external-relationship specialties. Composition is engagement-specific. The Board is yours for the duration.

Quarterly Strategic Reviews.

04
Annual Business Reviews.
Full strategic refresh. Recommitment to direction. Recalibration as conditions change. Twelve months looked at, twelve months ahead planned.
05
True Ownership™ at the highest altitude.
The 5A Framework — All In It, Align With It, Advocate For It, Act On It, Accountable To It — as the operating discipline of the entire engagement.
06
Year-after ongoing advisory.
The Board can transition into a governing Board, or stay as a strategic advisory layer. The relationship continues as long as the owner wants it to.
